# AI Copilot

The Assistant IA (`/assistant`) answers accounting and fiscal questions in French.

## Onboarding questions

- "Comment créer ma première facture ?"
- "Comment configurer la TVA ?"
- "Comment générer une liasse ?"

The copilot provides numbered steps and links to relevant modules.

## Context

Responses use your active company data when available. Always validate fiscal obligations with your expert-comptable.
